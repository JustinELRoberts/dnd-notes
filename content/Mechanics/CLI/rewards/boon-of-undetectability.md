# Boon of Undetectability

You gain a +10 bonus to Dexterity ([Stealth](/Mechanics/CLI/skills.md#Stealth)) checks, and you can't be detected or targeted by divination magic, including scrying sensors.

## Sources

_Dungeon Master's Guide p. 232_
