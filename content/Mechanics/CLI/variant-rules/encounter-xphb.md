# Encounter

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 366. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_

An encounter is a scene in an adventure that is part of at least one of the game's three pillars: social interaction, exploration, or combat.
