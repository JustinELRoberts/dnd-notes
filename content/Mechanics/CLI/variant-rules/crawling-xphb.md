# Crawling

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 364. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_

While you're crawling, each foot of movement costs 1 extra foot (2 extra feet in [difficult terrain](/Mechanics/CLI/variant-rules/difficult-terrain-xphb.md)).
