# Seeking Bomb

By replacing your [Bomb's](/Mechanics/CLI/items/bomb-valdaspire24.md) explosive contents with a powerful lodestone, you can change it into a Seeking Bomb. Your ranged attack with this [Bomb](/Mechanics/CLI/items/bomb-valdaspire24.md) and its explosion ignores [Half Cover](/Mechanics/CLI/variant-rules/cover-xphb.md) and [Three-Quarters Cover](/Mechanics/CLI/variant-rules/cover-xphb.md).
