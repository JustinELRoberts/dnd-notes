# Land Mermaid

The name is wrong and everyone who used it knew better. The job board in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Vel Rau'dan/index|Vel Rau'dan]] posted it as a "land mermaid" problem south of [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Myrrh's Hollow/index|Myrrh’s Hollow]]; [[Jimmothy]] pointed out there is no such thing. What was actually holding the [[Blue Cross Bridge]] was a siren.

## Origin

It was a man once. He lived under the [[Blue Cross Bridge]], and something there turned him. Searching the lair afterward, it was clear the thing had evolved multiple times to reach the state we fought — it did not arrive finished.

## The Song

Its song carried far past the bridge. Everyone in the region heard it, and those who failed against it did not recover: [[The Crazy One]] was left with [[madness]] a history check told us would last years, and he believed the song was why his son killed his daughter.

At the bridge itself the song forced Wisdom saving throws. Every civilian present failed and was charmed into walking off the bridge; the whole party passed. Mid-fight it also charmed [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]] and [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] into attacking each other.

The reach is at least partly explained by its ears. It was wearing [[The Earrings of Control]], one of the [[12 Artifacts]] — "the more powerful the user, the more powerful and vast the control can be." We cut them off its body.

## Lair

It had walled itself into the barricade across the [[Blue Cross Bridge]] — 50 feet high, 10 feet deep, opened by a rope like a door. Guards were turning travelers away on both sides, and [[Myrrh’s Hollow South]] had been cut off for about a week. Everything inside was covered in slime.
