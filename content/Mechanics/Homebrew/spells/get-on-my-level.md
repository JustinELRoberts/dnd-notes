# Get On My Level

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action (1/Day)
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** Unknown
- **Duration:** 1 minute

The caster points at one creature within 60 feet and forcibly drags its power down to his own level. For 1 minute, that creature's effective Challenge Rating becomes equal to the caster's CR.

If the creature normally has a higher CR than the caster, then while affected:

- Its attack bonus cannot exceed the caster's spell attack bonus.
- Its saving throw DCs cannot exceed the caster's spell save DC.
- Its AC cannot exceed the caster's AC.
- Any damage resistance or immunity the caster does not possess becomes resistance instead of immunity.
- Legendary Resistance uses are reduced to a maximum of 1 for the duration.

The creature keeps all of its normal attacks, spells, traits, and personality — it is simply forced to fight at the caster's level.

The effect ends early if the caster is Incapacitated or reduced to 0 HP.

> "Get on my level."
