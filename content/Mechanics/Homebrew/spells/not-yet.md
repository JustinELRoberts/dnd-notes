# Not Yet

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** Mythic (1/Day), when reduced to 0 HP
- **Range:** Self (60-foot radius)
- **Components:** Unknown
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

When the Piper would be reduced to 0 HP, the performance refuses to end. He immediately returns to his full 285 HP. He also:

- Ends all conditions affecting him.
- Recharges [[follow-my-lead|Follow My Lead]].
- Recharges Play, Piper, Play.
- Regains all spent Legendary Actions.
- Moves up to his Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

Then every creature within 60 feet that can hear him makes a **DC 17** Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it gains 1 [[master-of-the-melody|Melody Mark]]. Phantom instruments erupt around the battlefield as the entire song begins again.

> "You don't stop the song just because you didn't like the ending."

## Sources

_Homebrew. An ability of [[The Piper]]. Used in [[14-The Piper]] when we brought him to 0 HP, returning him to full._
