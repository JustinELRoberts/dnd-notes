# Whispered Verse

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** Legendary action (Cost 1)
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** Unknown
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

One creature within 60 feet makes a **DC 17** Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it gains 1 [[master-of-the-melody|Melody Mark]].
