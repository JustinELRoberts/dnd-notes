# Follow My Lead

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action (Recharge)
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** Unknown
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Up to six creatures within 60 feet that can hear the Piper make a **DC 17** Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the Piper moves the creature up to half its Speed in a direction of his choice. This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks. The creature also gains 1 [[master-of-the-melody|Melody Mark]].

## Sources

_Homebrew. An ability of [[The Piper]], from the fight in [[14-The Piper]]._
