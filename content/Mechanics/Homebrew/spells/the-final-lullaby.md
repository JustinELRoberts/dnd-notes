# The Final Lullaby

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** Unknown
- **Duration:** 1 minute

Every creature of the Piper's choice within 120 feet that can hear him makes a **DC 17** Wisdom saving throw. Creatures with 3 [[master-of-the-melody|Melody Mark]]s have Disadvantage on this save.

On a failed save, the creature falls [Unconscious](/Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Unconscious) for 1 minute. It wakes if:

- It takes damage.
- Another creature uses an Action to wake it.
- The Piper becomes [Incapacitated](/Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Incapacitated).

A creature that succeeds is immune to The Final Lullaby for the rest of the encounter.

## Sources

_Homebrew. An ability of [[The Piper]], from the fight in [[14-The Piper]]._
