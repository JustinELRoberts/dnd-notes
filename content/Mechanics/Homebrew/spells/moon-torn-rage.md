# Moon-Torn Rage

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus action (2/Long Rest)
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** Unknown
- **Duration:** 1 minute

[[Roz Rozum|Roz]] gives himself completely to the beast for 1 minute. While raging:

- He has Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
- His melee attacks deal an extra `1d8` Slashing damage.
- His Speed increases by 10 feet.
- He has Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.
- He may still cast and concentrate on [[cleric-xphb|Cleric]] spells.

The rage ends early if Roz is Incapacitated or chooses to end it.

## Sources

_Homebrew. [[Roz Rozum|Roz]]'s [[weretiger]] rage. Used in [[13-Full Moon#The full moon]], where losing control on the full moon turned it on [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]]. Written as an ability rather than a castable spell._
