# Crescendo

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** Legendary action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** Unknown
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Every creature within 30 feet that has at least one [[master-of-the-melody|Melody Mark]] makes a **DC 17** Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it gains 1 Melody Mark.

## Sources

_Homebrew. An ability of [[The Piper]], from the fight in [[14-The Piper]]._
