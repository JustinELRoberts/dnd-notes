# Otto's Irresistible Dance (Piper)

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** Legendary action (Cost 1, once per round)
- **Range:** As [Otto's Irresistible Dance](/Mechanics/CLI/spells/ottos-irresistible-dance-xphb.md)
- **Components:** None
- **Duration:** As the spell, without Concentration

The Piper casts [Otto's Irresistible Dance](/Mechanics/CLI/spells/ottos-irresistible-dance-xphb.md) on one creature within range. He does not require Concentration on the spell when cast this way.

A creature currently affected by [[you-belong-to-the-song|You Belong to the Song]] cannot be targeted.

## Sources

_Homebrew. An ability of [[The Piper]], from the fight in [[14-The Piper]]._
