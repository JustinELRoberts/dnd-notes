# Wrong Note

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction, when the Piper is hit by an attack
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** Unknown
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

One creature within 30 feet that can hear the Piper makes a **DC 17** Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, if the creature is a legal target for the triggering attack, the Piper redirects the attack to it instead.

## Sources

_Homebrew. An ability of [[The Piper]], from the fight in [[14-The Piper]]._
