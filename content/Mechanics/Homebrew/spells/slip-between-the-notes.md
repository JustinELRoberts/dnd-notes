# Slip Between the Notes

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction, when a creature ends its movement within 5 feet of the Piper
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** Unknown
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The Piper moves up to 15 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

## Sources

_Homebrew. An ability of [[The Piper]], from the fight in [[14-The Piper]]._
