# Change The Tempo

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** Unknown
- **Duration:** Until the start of the Piper's next turn

The Piper chooses one effect:

- **Allegro.** His Speed increases by 20 feet.
- **Adagio.** Ranged attacks made against him from more than 30 feet away have Disadvantage.
- **Crescendo.** The first creature that fails a saving throw against him gains 1 additional [[master-of-the-melody|Melody Mark]].

## Sources

_Homebrew. An ability of [[The Piper]], from the fight in [[14-The Piper]]._
