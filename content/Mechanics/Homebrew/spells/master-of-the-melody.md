# Master of the Melody

_Unknown level, Unknown school_

- **Casting time:** Trait (passive)
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** None
- **Duration:** Permanent

Whenever a creature fails a saving throw against one of the Piper's Charm or musical abilities, it gains 1 Melody Mark.

- **1 Mark:** The song has entered the creature's mind.
- **2 Marks:** The creature has Disadvantage on the next Wisdom saving throw it makes against the Piper.
- **3 Marks:** The creature becomes [Charmed](/Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Charmed) by the Piper for 1 minute.

A creature Charmed this way repeats a **DC 17** Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a success, the Charm ends and all Melody Marks are removed.

A [Deafened](/Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Deafened) creature has Advantage on saving throws against the Piper's musical effects, but is not immune.

## Sources

_Homebrew. An ability of [[The Piper]], from the fight in [[14-The Piper]]._
