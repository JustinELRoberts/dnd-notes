1/Day — Action The Apprentice points at one creature within 60 feet and forcibly drags its power down to his own level. For 1 minute, that creature's effective Challenge Rating becomes equal to the Blue Wizard Apprentice's CR. If the creature normally has a higher CR than the Apprentice, while affected: Its attack bonus cannot exceed the Apprentice's spell attack bonus. Its saving throw DCs cannot exceed the Apprentice's Spell Save DC. Its AC cannot exceed the Apprentice's AC. Any damage resistance or immunity the Apprentice does not possess becomes resistance instead of immunity. Legendary Resistance uses are reduced to a maximum of 1 for the duration. The creature keeps all of its normal attacks, spells, traits, and personality—it is simply forced to fight at the Apprentice's level. The effect ends early if the Apprentice is Incapacitated or reduced to 0 HP. “Get on my level.”
