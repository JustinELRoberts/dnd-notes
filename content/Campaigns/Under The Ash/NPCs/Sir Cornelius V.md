## Description

The [[half-orc]] behind the counter at [[The Sabertooth Tavern]] in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Virelya/Carrowyn/index|Carrowyn]], usually playing with his pet bird [[Bird]]. He cooks, he is friendly, and he talks more than he means to.
In [[13-Full Moon#At The Sabertooth Tavern]] he let slip that people don't often eat "on this side of the tavern", then went quiet about it. The other side is the sugar circle down the stairs and everything behind the padlocked door.
