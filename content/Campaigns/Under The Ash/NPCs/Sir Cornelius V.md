## Description

The [[half-orc]] behind the counter at [[The Sabertooth Tavern]] in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Virelya/Carrowyn/index|Carrowyn]], usually playing with his pet bird [[Bird]]. Friendly, and talks more than he means to.
In [[13-Full Moon#At The Sabertooth Tavern]] he let slip that people don't often eat "on this side of the tavern" — the other side being the [[Feywild]] portal downstairs.
His tavern burned down in [[14-The Piper]] during our fight with [[The Piper]].
