## Description

The barkeep at [[The Iron Wake Mug]], and now the manager of [[The Tail's End]]. She agreed to it in [[10-Meeting Rithas#At The Iron Wake Mug]].

She has been feeding us information about [[Theratina]]. She lent [[Raelor]] [[The Human King of the Elves]], later gave him [[The Land That Was Taken Quietly]] because the story matches [[Theratina]]'s, and does not think [[King Caelen Thorne]] had a second child.

[[V]] implied she had killed her on the [[Blue Cross Bridge]]. She hadn't — Diana sent [[Raelor]] a [[sending-xphb|Sending]] at the end of [[12-The Executioner#On Ferrar's Airship (again)]] saying she has started hiring.
