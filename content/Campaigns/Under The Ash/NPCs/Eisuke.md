## Description

A roughly 4 foot tall [[tabaxi-mpmm|Tabaxi]] with two tails, red eyes, plum colored fur, and a full head of hair. He showed up confused at the bar of [[The Tail's End]] in [[11-Land Mermaid#At The Tail's End]].
[[Callie Dawnsong|Cal]] dug through his memories and confirmed he is from 190 years in our future. On their word, we let him travel with us.
He appears and vanishes without warning; he disappeared crossing the [[Blue Cross Bridge]] and again as we left [[The Shaded Cavern]].

## What we know

- He knew what [[The Executioner]] was on sight
- He recognized [[The Realm Jumper]] in [[The Mirror Of What Is]]
- He has never heard of [[Myrrh’s Hollow]], which suggests it doesn't survive the coming war
- The [[Three Wizards]] spoke to him and his group in a dream; that is what started their quest
