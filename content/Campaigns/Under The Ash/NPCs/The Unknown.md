## Description

A force of evil that exists in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/index]]. Many years ago, every nation put aside their differences to fight The Unknown. Ultimately the people prevailed; The Unknown is now locked away behind 12 locks; each city holds the key to one of these locks.

We have seen him once. Calling to the [[Coin of The Unknown]] took us to the [[Dimensional Blue Arena]] in [[09-Avenj's Death#At The Dimensional Blue Arena]], where he stood in front of us as a moving mountain of shadow and [[power-word-kill-xphb|Power Word Kill]]ed [[Avenj]] without a word of warning.

> Let this be a lesson. You are lucky I take only him. You are nothing more than ants. I step on ants.
