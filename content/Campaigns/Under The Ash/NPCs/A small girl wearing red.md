## Description

A small girl in a red dress, out back of [[The Laughing Root]]. She stopped us from leaving in [[13-Full Moon#In the Feywild]], pointed out that our bread was missing, and made it clear she knows a great deal about us.
She claims the [[The Hag|Hag]] we killed was hers.
She says [[V]] is the one who put her there, and offered to tell us everything about her. We did not make a deal.

## What we know

- [[Rithas]] knows her and is elusive about it. She apparently helped him get to this plane
- She is stuck at [[The Laughing Root]], or at least she behaves as though she is

## Motivations

- Get out, and get us to make a deal to do it
