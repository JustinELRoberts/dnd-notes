## Description

[[Roz Rozum|Roz]]'s shield. He has a face that animates when [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] knocks and rolls well enough. In life he was a [[genasi-water-mpmm|Genasi (Water)]]; [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] saw that form when he died at the arena.

Every time he closes his eyes he is in heaven with [[forgotten-realms-selune|Selûne]], who gave him to [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] on purpose.

He is also a party line — other gods borrow his face. [[forgotten-realms-malar|Malar]] in [[05-Shopping Spree#A conversation with Malar]], [[dawn-war-asmodeus-dmg|Asmodeus]] in [[09-Avenj's Death#A conversation with Jimmothy]]. He doesn't get a choice about sharing [[Roz Rozum|Roz]]'s secrets with them.

## What we know

- He does not trust the lycanthropes ([[Ferrar]], [[Thompson]], and so on)
- He knew about the rumor of a [[Three Wizards]] apprentice at [[The Shaded Cavern]], which is what sent us to [[The Blue Apprentice]]
