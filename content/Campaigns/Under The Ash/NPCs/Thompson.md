## Description

Brother of [[Ferrar]] and leader of [[The Dark Beasts]]. Due to their familial connection, he can evidently steal [[Ferrar]]'s alpha status if he bites a pre-existing lycanthrope.

He slaughtered every person in [[The Unnamed City|The Border City]], limb by limb, and left one two year old alive by accident. His blood ran purple — refined [[Tears of Silvanus]].

[[V]] took [[The Stoneheart]] off him in [[09-Avenj's Death#Inside the house]] and left him sick in bed with his missing hand: "I don't believe you deserve this knife anymore. It has corrupted you in a way you said it wouldn't."

A [[weretiger]] in his own house killed him minutes later. Every lycanthrope guarding the camp turned back to human on the spot and begged for mercy; he had taken their free will.
