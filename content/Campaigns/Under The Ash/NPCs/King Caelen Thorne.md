## Description

Ruler of [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Vel Rau'dan/index|Vel Rau'dan]]; the man who ended the [[Elven Civil War]] with words instead of blades. His story is the one told in [[The Human King of the Elves]] and [[Crown of Thorns, Crown of Ash]].
In [[06-Getting Rich#Raelor's conversation with Diana]], [[Raelor]] asked [[Iron Wake Mug Barkeep|Diana]] whether he could have had a second child. She doesn't think so.
