## Description

An apprentice of the blue member of the [[Three Wizards]], living alone in a house at [[The Shaded Cavern]]. He refused to give us his name and told us to call him this instead.
He was extremely nervous that we were there at all. He kept repeating that we were supposed to be in [[Carrowyn]], that he isn't supposed to be in "our story", and that he knows how "the story" is supposed to go. I repeatedly got the vibe that he is a timeline manager of some kind.
He said he couldn't fight [[The Executioner]] for us but could "make it even", then cast [[Get On My Level]] on it so we could kill it.

## What we know

- He says the [[Three Wizards]] are the ones who know exactly what is going to happen
- He warned that [[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]] dies later if we go, and that [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] kills [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]] on the full moon if we stay
