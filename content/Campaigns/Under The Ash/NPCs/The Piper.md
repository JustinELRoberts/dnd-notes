## Description

A master of hypnotism. He travels by boat with a crowd of rodents, spellcasters, and bards playing for him while he dances like a madman. We filled our ears with wax before meeting him.

He arrived at [[Thompson's Camp]] mid-standoff in [[09-Avenj's Death#Confronting V]], exactly as [[dawn-war-asmodeus-dmg|Asmodeus]] said he would. [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] passed him the message with [[message-xphb|Message]].

> Thank you good sir. I'm off to find a dragon.
> He turned up again at [[The Sabertooth Tavern]] in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Virelya/Carrowyn/index|Carrowyn]] at the very end of [[13-Full Moon#The full moon]], walking in and looking directly at [[Theratina]].

In [[14-The Piper]] he demanded either the [[notebook with a sleeping face on its cover|notebook]] or [[Theratina]], then fought us. He survived being dropped to 0 HP, stole the notebook by rewinding time, and [[teleport-xphb|Teleport]]ed away with 20 followers who had been waiting outside. His [[fireball-xphb|Fireball]] burned [[The Sabertooth Tavern]] down.

> I know who you all are. I understand what you all have. I'm trying to do something, so I'd appreciate it if you helped.

- [[master-of-the-melody|Master of the Melody]] — failed saves against his charms and music give Melody Marks; 3 marks means Charmed. Being Deafened gives Advantage
- Actions: [[follow-my-lead|Follow My Lead]], [[the-final-lullaby|The Final Lullaby]]
- Bonus Actions: [[you-belong-to-the-song|You Belong to the Song]], [[change-the-tempo|Change The Tempo]]
- Reactions: [[wrong-note|Wrong Note]], [[slip-between-the-notes|Slip Between the Notes]], [[silence-the-wrong-note|Silence the Wrong Note]]
- Legendary: [[ottos-irresistible-dance-piper|Otto's Irresistible Dance]], [[whispered-verse|Whispered Verse]], [[crescendo|Crescendo]]
- Mythic: [[not-yet|Not Yet]] — 1/day, returns to full HP at 0 HP. We can't win by damage alone
- [[song-into-the-past|Song Into the Past]] — once per year; he used it to steal the notebook
