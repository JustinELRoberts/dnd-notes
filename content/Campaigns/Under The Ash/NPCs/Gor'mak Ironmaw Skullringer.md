## Description

A [[troll]] with many titles. His full name is Gor'mak Ironmaw Skullrender, Son of Kharzug the Unbroken. Also Bearer of the World Axe, Warlord of the Broken Host, Keeper of the Old Oaths, Butcher of the Crimson Vale, Champion of the Red Moon Lord of the Iron Horde, Chosen of Gruumsh Slayer of Seven Kings Guardian of the First Banner, Last Kin of the Black Crags, Warden of the Ember March, Walker of Endless Wars, Shield of the Mountain Clans, and Conqueror of the Thousand Fields.
