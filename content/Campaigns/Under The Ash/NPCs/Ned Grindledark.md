## Description

He is the creator of the [[Scary Frankenstein Guy|stitched boy]] but lost him.

He worked with [[Thompson]] and [[Ferrar]] on supersoldier experiments, using books of heroes like _The Champions of Evermore_ as references. He is also the [[The Grindledarks|Grindledark]] hunting [[Theratina]].

[[V]] wrote him [[The Letter to Grindledark]], telling him to gather [[The Earrings of Control]], [[The Ring of Endless Power]], and [[The Necklace of Life]], and promising his son could be brought back. We intercepted it at the post office in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Vel Rau'dan/index|Vel Rau'dan]]; he has not read it.

## Motivations

- He wants to find the [[Scary Frankenstein Guy|stitched boy]]
- Find [[Theratina]]
- Bring his son back
