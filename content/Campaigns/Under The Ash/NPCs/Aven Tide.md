## Description

One of the victims of [[The Hag]] who pretended to be an innkeeper at [[Starlight Inn|The Starlight Inn]].
Her portrait hangs over the fireplace at [[The Laughing Root]] in the [[Feywild]]. The server called her "a friend, a dear friend", and said they hadn't seen her in a little while, though time there passes strangely.
