## Description

An alpha werewolf and part of [[The Red Maw|The Red Maw]]. He keeps [[Glasscap Grove]] in [[Moon Hollow]], where he claims to shelter 45,000 wolves plus widows, orphans, and people displaced by war.

He sponsored us at the [[Arena of Vel'Therin Expanse]] for 10% of our winnings and pays for our [[resurrection-xphb|Resurrection]]s. He cursed [[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]] ([[werewolf]]) and [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] ([[weretiger]]) at their request, then released them from the binding.

[[Thompson]] was his brother. He was willing to watch him die but couldn't do it himself, and carried a locket with the two of them in it. [[Thompson]] turned him to stone with [[The Stoneheart]] at [[The Unnamed City|The Border City]].

We have had [[Ferrar's Airship]] since. He keeps finding us on the road with it.

- He worked with [[Thompson]] and someone matching [[Ned Grindledark]] on supersoldier experiments, referencing books of heroes like _The Champions of Evermore_
