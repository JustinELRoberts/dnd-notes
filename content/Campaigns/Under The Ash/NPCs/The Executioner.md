## Description

One of [[The Unknown]]'s soldiers. 15 feet tall and wrapped in chains, fast enough that it hits you before you ever see it.
[[V]] sent it after us in [[12-The Executioner#In the basement]] as punishment for interrupting her ritual. She offered a day's head start and then cut it to twelve hours.
When it arrived at [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Myrrh's Hollow/index|Myrrh’s Hollow]] the sound it made was heard across the entire [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/index|Vel’Therin Expanse]] — [[Jimmothy]] says they heard it in the heavens too.
We fought and killed it outside [[The Shaded Cavern]] with the help of [[The Blue Apprentice]]. When it died it turned into the same goo we saw from the [[Coin of The Unknown]].

## Motivations

- Kill whoever [[The Unknown]] (or [[V]]) points it at
