## Description

A coming red moon. Everything we hear points to it being the hinge the rest of this turns on.

## What we know

- The [[Scary Frankenstein Guy|stitched agent]] warned us in [[00-First Session#The Ambush and the Stitched Agent]]: "Enjoy life while you still have it. When the moon shines red, as the color of this diamond, all Hell will begin to break loose"
- Per the pirate captain in [[08-One Shot Ale Hunt#Interrogating the Captain]], [[V]] is working with [[The Council of the Cold Veil|The Council]] to _stop_ the blood moon
- [[forgotten-realms-selune|Selûne]] told [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] in [[10-Meeting Rithas#Roz's prayer]]: "There's a lot that is going to happen. It is all coming close as the Blood Moon grows near"
