## Description

The war that made [[King Caelen Thorne]] the Human King of the Elves, told in [[The Human King of the Elves]] and [[Crown of Thorns, Crown of Ash]].
When the old elven king of [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Vel Rau'dan/index|Vel Rau'dan]] died, [[Thalan’dor]] marched to take the throne, led by Prince Vaereth, who had wanted to marry the king's daughter Aelira. She chose Caelen instead, a human knight from [[Brackenmill]]. Caelen killed Vaereth in battle and Thalan'dor was pushed back.

## What we know

- The official story says Caelen ended the war "with words instead of blades"; Vaereth's war journal says otherwise
- Per the children's book, the old king got his power from a deal with a witch for his firstborn, and fell to her curse when Aelira turned eighteen
- Its scars still run deep in the [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/index|Vel’Therin Expanse]]
