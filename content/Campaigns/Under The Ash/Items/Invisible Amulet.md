## Description

An amulet with a heart intentionally broken in half; each half holds a vial of a different type of fluid. The heart itself is gold and the chain is made of platinum.

It was invisible until [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] spotted it with [[see-invisibility-xphb|See Invisibility]] and stole it from [[The Card-Jutsu]] in [[01-Meeting Cal]] when he passed out drunk. [[The Card-Jutsu]] was waiting for [[Ned Grindledark]], so it may be connected to him.

## Uses

- A spying device. [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]] cast [[identify-xphb|Identify]] on it in [[05-Shopping Spree#At The Iron Wake Mug]]: it has a clone, and uses abjuration magic to pick up sound and transmit it somewhere else
