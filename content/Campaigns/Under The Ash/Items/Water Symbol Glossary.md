## Description

Translated fragments of the Tidekeeper tablets, giving the word each symbol on the [[Water Symbol Stone]] stands for. Its margins warn that position matters as much as translation, and that the moon decides which symbols may speak.
Handed to [[Raelor]] with the [[Water Symbol Stone]] by the [[gnome]]s at the [[CFN Headquarters]] in [[13-Full Moon#At the CFN Headquarters]].

## Uses

- The key to reading the [[Water Symbol Stone]]
