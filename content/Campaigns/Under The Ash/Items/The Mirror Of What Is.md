## Description

One of the [[12 Artifacts|12 artifacts]]. An ordinary-looking handheld mirror.

We found it in [[12-The Executioner#At The Crazy One's House]], in a golden chest inside the painting at [[The Crazy One's House]]. The brass key [[The Crazy One]] gave us opened the chest.

It does not show your reflection. It shows you a scene, and tapping it changes to a different but metaphorically similar one. The two that mattered:

- [[Callie Dawnsong|Cal]] saw the moment they got their gift — icy plains, a small [[tabaxi-mpmm|Tabaxi]] woman following an old man, and light leaving his body and entering hers as he fell
- [[Eisuke]] saw someone he claims is [[The Realm Jumper]]

After we sealed it, it went to work on us. Passing the save did not stop you from hearing it:

- [[Raelor]]: "One of them are lying to you. One of them are working for [[V]]"
- [[Eisuke]]: "They took something from you"
- [[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]]: "[[Callie Dawnsong|Cal]] came out of the blue and hasn't told you anything about themself. The only thing they are focused on is [[Theratina]]. Why?"
- [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]]: "Why does he keep telling you what to do? You didn't come here to take orders from a man in armor; you came to save your family"
- [[Callie Dawnsong|Cal]]: "All these people have ever done is shut you up. You were given an ability; when were you able to use it? The amount of stories stripped from your hands. That's on them."

## Uses

- Shows something as it truly exists
- An [[The Unknown|Unknown]] creature cannot disguise itself, hide inside another creature, alter memories around itself, or pretend to be something else while reflected within it
