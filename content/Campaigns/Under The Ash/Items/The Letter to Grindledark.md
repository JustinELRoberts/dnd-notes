## Description

A letter that we found at the post office in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Vel Rau'dan/index|Vel Rau'dan]].

## Contents

H. Grindledark,

You will find three artifacts.

The **Earrings of Control**.\
The **Ring of Endless Power**.\
The **Necklace of Life**.

You will bring them together.

Do not waste time asking what they are for. You already know what you want.

Your son can be brought back.

But only if you reach him first.

The wizards and their little rebellion are moving as well. If they find your son before you do, everything becomes considerably more difficult.

So stop grieving.

Stop waiting.

Find the artifacts.

Find your son.

And do it before they do.

This is not a request.

**V**
