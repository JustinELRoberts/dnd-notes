## Description

A notebook with a sleeping face on its cover. There are three riddles needed to open it:

1. I did not write this one fast enough :(
   - The answer is "death"
2. "Before the first question, I am there. After the last answer, I remain. Scholars chase me through dusty pages. Kings fear the shape I might take. Name me, and you think you understand me. Yet the moment you do… I become something else. What am I?"
   - The answer is "the unknown"
3. "I am the lie that keeps the living breathing, the whisper that defies the grave. I grow strongest when the world grows darkest, yet I cannot exist without despair. I have no weight, yet armies march because of me. I have no voice, yet the hopeless pray for me. Break me… and hearts fall silent. Keep me… and even the doomed will fight. What am I?"
   - The answer is "hope"

Per [[Bruce]] in [[02-The Hag#Bruce Examines the Book]], it is one of the most powerful pocket portals ever made, created by the [[Three Wizards]] to seal the [[12 Artifacts]]. It has more than 12 pages; see [[12 Artifacts]].

The pages are blank until an artifact is placed inside. Each one sealed reveals a page describing it and a blurry image of the next to find; see [[Seal the 12 Artifacts]] for the order so far.

[[Avenj]]'s last words before his spirit departed were not to let [[The Council of the Cold Veil|Council of the Cold Veil]] get this book.

**Stolen.** [[The Piper]] took it from us in [[14-The Piper]] using [[song-into-the-past|Song Into the Past]], then [[teleport-xphb|Teleport]]ed away. Whereabouts unknown.

## Uses

- Used to seal the [[12 Artifacts]] (by placing them inside it)
- Vibrates in the presence of an unsealed artifact
- Artifacts remain removable; you can reach into the page and pull one back out
