## Description

The lower of the two stones. Fourteen symbols around its face, one per letter of the answer.
The [[gnome]]s at the [[CFN Headquarters]] handed it to [[Raelor]] with the [[Water Symbol Glossary]] in [[13-Full Moon#At the CFN Headquarters]], after we agreed to finish the puzzle they could not. It clinks when you take it out at [[The Laughing Root]].
Joined with the [[Water Hour Stone]], the two open onto a latch with a dial and a fourteen letter slot. We entered "under water city" and got the [[steel and coral key]].
