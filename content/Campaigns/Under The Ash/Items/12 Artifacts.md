## Description

Twelve items created by [[The Unknown]] during his reign of terror. No creature can hold one for more than three days without dying, and humans corrupt them. They can be "sealed" by placing them into the [[notebook with a sleeping face on its cover|notebook]] (presumably negating the three day limit).

The [[notebook with a sleeping face on its cover|notebook]] has more than 12 pages. [[Callie Dawnsong|Cal]] recalls rumors that the [[Three Wizards]] had 12 artifacts of their own, which would make 24 total. [[Bruce]] did not seem to know this.

The items we have encountered so far are:

1. [[Diamond of 20 Monsters]]
2. [[Staff of Souls]]
3. [[The Stoneheart]]
4. [[The Earrings of Control]]
5. [[The Mirror Of What Is]]

The book has shown us [[The Necklace of Life]] as the next one to find. [[The Ring of Endless Power]] is named in [[The Letter to Grindledark]] but we have not seen it or its page.

The five sealed artifacts went with the [[notebook with a sleeping face on its cover|notebook]] when [[The Piper]] stole it in [[14-The Piper]].

See [[Seal the 12 Artifacts]] for who else is hunting them.
