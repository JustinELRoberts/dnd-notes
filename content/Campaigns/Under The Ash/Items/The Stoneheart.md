## Description

One of the [[12 Artifacts]], and one of the most powerful of them. It is a massive, mangled dagger far too thick and long for a normal human to wield.

[[Thompson]] carried it until [[V]] decided he no longer deserved it — "It has corrupted you in a way you said it wouldn't." She handed it to [[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]] specifically to see how fast we would corrupt. We sealed it in the [[notebook with a sleeping face on its cover]] instead; see [[09-Avenj's Death#Confronting V]].

## Uses

- We saw in [[07-Tavern Owners#The fight]] that when someone who is not attuned comes into contact with it, they turn to stone until that contact is lost
- Gives the user paralyzing magic and siphoning magic anywhere within 30 feet
  - It siphons the magic of the people around them
