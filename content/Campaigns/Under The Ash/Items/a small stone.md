## Description

Half of a puzzle. The [[gnome]]s at the [[CFN Headquarters]] handed it to [[Raelor]] along with a [[mysterious note]] in [[13-Full Moon#At the CFN Headquarters]], after we agreed to finish the puzzle they could not.
It clinks when you take it out at [[The Laughing Root]]. Its matching half was sitting behind [[The Lawnkeeper]] out back, and we took it.
Joined, the two open onto a latch with a dial and a slot that takes a fourteen letter answer. We entered "under water city" and it gave us the [[steel and coral key]].
