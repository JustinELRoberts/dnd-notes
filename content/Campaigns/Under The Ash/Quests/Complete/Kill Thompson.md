## Description

[[Bruce]] told us in [[06-Getting Rich]] that [[Thompson]], leader of [[The Dark Beasts]], is forcibly turning people into lycanthropes, and that killing him would cure everyone he infected. He likes to keep outposts near his den and isolates himself when threatened.
We tracked him to [[Thompson's Camp]] in [[09-Avenj's Death]] with [[dawn-war-asmodeus-dmg|Asmodeus]]'s help, but a [[weretiger]] killed him before we could. His followers were freed on the spot.
