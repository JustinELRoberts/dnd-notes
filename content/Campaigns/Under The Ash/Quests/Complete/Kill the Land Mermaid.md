## Description

A job board posting in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Vel Rau'dan/index|Vel Rau'dan]] about "land mermaids" south of [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Myrrh's Hollow/index|Myrrh’s Hollow]]. The man at the board was confused we were even taking it, and told us only one person had ever seen them: [[The Crazy One]].
[[Jimmothy]] pointed out there is no such thing as a land mermaid. He was right — it was a siren, and it had made its lair inside the barricade across the [[Blue Cross Bridge]].
