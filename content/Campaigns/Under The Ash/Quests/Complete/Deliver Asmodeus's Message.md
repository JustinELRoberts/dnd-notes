## Description

[[Roz Rozum|Roz]] asked the gods through [[Jimmothy]] where [[Thompson]] was and got [[dawn-war-asmodeus-dmg|Asmodeus]] instead. In exchange for the answer, he wanted a message carried to [[The Piper]], promising we would "coincidentally" run into him.
We did. [[The Piper]] arrived on a boat at [[Thompson's Camp]] mid-standoff, and [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] passed the message along with [[message-xphb|Message]].

> Thank you good sir. I'm off to find a dragon.
> The dragon is almost certainly [[Drakhakar]] in [[Grumdur-Khaz]].
