## Description

Find the [[12 Artifacts]] and seal them in the [[notebook with a sleeping face on its cover]] before anyone else gets them. Each time we seal one, the book shows us the next.
We are not the only ones hunting them. [[V]] is collecting them to awaken [[The Unknown]], and she admitted in [[12-The Executioner#In the basement]] that she has been using us to do the collecting for her. [[The Council of the Cold Veil]] wants them locked in the vault at [[Caer Thalor]], and [[Avenj]]'s dying warning was not to let them have the book.
