- After an hour, we reach the aforementioned stream

  - There are very big, humanoid footprints on the other side
- A huge creature approaches on the other side of the stream

- It appears to be some sort of [[troll]] seeking a toll to cross
  - His name is [[Gor'mak Ironmaw Skullringer]]...
    - Theres like 500 titles
- [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] uses [[mold-earth-xge|Mold Earth]] to destroy the bridge
- [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] offers to rebuild it once we cross, assuming people we designate can cross unharmed
  - He also offers to let him pet "the biggest cat you've ever seen"

  - It is a bunch of cats with cigars at a table playing poker
- [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] transforms into his lycanthrope form (a [[weretiger]]) and lets the troll pet him
  - [[Avenj]] uses a flask of [[enlarge-reduce-xphb|Enlarge/Reduce]] to make him bigger

- It is extremely foggy/misty around us

  - [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]] recognizes it is caused by some kind of illusion magic

  - Abjuration is in there, too, to regenerate itself
- We decide to [[dispel-magic-xphb|Dispel Magic]] and make a run for it
