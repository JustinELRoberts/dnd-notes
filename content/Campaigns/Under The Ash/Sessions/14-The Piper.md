## Summary

[[The Piper]] walked into [[The Sabertooth Tavern]] knowing exactly who we were and what we carried, and asked for either the [[notebook with a sleeping face on its cover|notebook]] or [[Theratina]]. His [[mass-suggestion-xphb|Mass Suggestion]] failed and the fight started. We dropped him to 0 HP once, but [[not-yet|Not Yet]] brought him straight back to full, so [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]] and [[Callie Dawnsong|Cal]] fled with [[Theratina]] through the sugar circle to the [[Feywild]]. It didn't matter: [[song-into-the-past|Song Into the Past]] let him rewind a few seconds and take the notebook before they left. He walked out to 20 followers waiting outside and [[teleport-xphb|Teleport]]ed away, leaving the tavern burning down from his [[fireball-xphb|Fireball]]. As the session ended, six people with baby dragons on their shoulders approached [[Roz Rozum|Roz]], [[Rithas]], and [[Raelor]].
