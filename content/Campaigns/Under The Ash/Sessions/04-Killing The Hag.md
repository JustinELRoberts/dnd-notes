## Summary

The high-stakes standoff with [[The Hag]] concluded as [[Raelor]] successfully broke [[Theratina]]’s trance, forcing the monstrous crone to slip into the Ethereal Plane using her [[heartstone-mm|Heartstone]]. Left behind in a heavily illusioned, trap-ridden lair, the party solved a cryptic knocking puzzle to infiltrate her inner sanctum. Along the way, they slaughtered a hidden [[Oni]] carrying an adamantine coin marked with the ancient [[Symbol of the Unknown]], and uncovered a horrific trophy room of past victims. After rejecting [[The Hag]]'s final bargain, the party secured a crystal containing the captured [[young-blue-dragon|Young Blue Dragon]] and claimed the immensely powerful [[Staff of Souls]]. Among the spoils was the exact book [[Iron Wake Mug Barkeep|Diana]] requested: [[Crown of Thorns, Crown of Ash]]. As the party escaped, the looming danger surrounding [[Theratina]]'s true identity became chillingly clear, though the harrowing ordeal earned the party a well-deserved **Level Up to 6**.

## Events

### Bypassing the Illusionary Door

- **The Standoff:** [[The Hag]] demands we leave [[Theratina]] behind in exchange for our lives. [[Raelor]] maintains a firm physical grip on [[Theratina]], successfully snapping her out of the magical trance.
- **The Escape:** Rather than fight directly, [[The Hag]] attempts to barter for her staff before vanishing completely into the Ethereal Plane.
- **The Puzzle:** The northern exit vanishes, replaced by a solid wall and a glowing red line. [[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]] and [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]] realize the entire room is an illusion. A single card sits on a table near markings:
  - 1 line — 3 dots
  - 2 lines — 4 dots
  - 3 lines — 1 dot
- **Trial and Error:** [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]] tries sliding the card into a slot to no avail. Knocking on the wall triggers various traps:
  - 1 knock or 4 knocks results in a booming, disembodied **"NOOO"**.
  - 3 knocks shoots a searing light out of the wall, damaging [[Avenj]]'s bone minion.
- **The Solution:** The party successfully opens the passage by executing the correct knocking sequence: **3 knocks, 3 knocks, 1 knock**.

### Clearing the Lair's Defenses

- The Den (To the Left)
  - Heavily rigged with a tripwire designed to shatter toxic vials collected from the corrupted farmlands.
  - [[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]] dismantles the trap and finds 2 vials of the Hag's poison (the same poison found in [[03-Battle Toads#At the farm]])
- The Kitchen
  - A horrific scene filled with children's bones alongside barrels and bottles overflowing with blood and unidentifiable sludge
- The Statue Room (To the East)
  - Features an altar slicked with blood, deep claw marks, various scrolls, and cryptic glyphs carved into the wall reading: _Open, Pull, Abominate_.
  - [[Raelor]] translates a scroll written in Orcish:
    > "I’ve been trying to get out for weeks. I don't know why she left me here. I don't know when she’ll wake up. She’s gone. I need to figure out how to get the portal to work. My necklace seems to burn the hag."

### Combat: The Oni

- Orc footprints lead down the northern hallway. Utilizing [[see-invisibility-xphb|See Invisibility]], [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] spots a hidden silver-chained necklace bearing the _Wrest of Shaloon_ (a holy symbol).
- A hidden monster screeches **"FEED ME"** and ambushes the party.
- **Combat:** We engage and defeat an [[Oni]]. Under the weight of [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]]'s attacks, chunks of the creature's body visibly fall apart.
- **The Loot:** Embedded in the [[oni|Oni]]'s right temple is an adamantine coin.
  - One side depicts a dark cloud with eyes.
  - The reverse side reads _"Call to me"_ in Common.
  - [[Avenj]] and [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]] recognize this as the [[Symbol of the Unknown]]—an ancient seal used long ago to lock away a catastrophic entity.

### The Inner Sanctum & Fighting [[The Hag]]

- The air grows heavy and suffocating in the Hag's personal quarters. Toxins continuously drip from the ceiling onto a central platform.
- [[The Hag]] sits on the platform. The door seals behind us, shifting back into a solid wall. She taunts us, asking how it felt to meet [[Anthony]] before laying down a final offer:
  > "I can get you the staff, and send you on your way. I saw your memories, I know what you want. I know what you’re looking for... You don’t know who she is. I do."
- The party adamantly refuses to hand over [[Theratina]].
- Realizing the deal is dead, [[The Hag]] enters a final phase and attacks [[Theratina]], who becomes unconscious
- Soon after, we defeat [[The Hag]]
- Despite casting healing spells etc on [[Theratina]], she remains unconscious (it seems she is immune)
- We try to encourage her to return to the light and just barely she awakens
- She tells us of a vision she had where she saw [[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]] (implying he was some sort of divine entity)
- We notice that she now is a bit older looking

### Lore on [[The Unknown]] & [[The Awoken]]

_Discovered via ancient books written in Black Speak left on the desk._

- [[The Awoken]]: The fanatical army serving [[The Unknown]], dedicated to ruling the planes through absolute fear.
- **The History:** While three ancient wizards were actively forging the locks to bind [[The Unknown]] away, a parallel faction of hunters was systematically tracking down members of [[The Awoken]].
- **The Cover-up:** Paranoid executioners wrongfully murdered countless innocents in the crossfire. Among the recorded historical victims were [[Cera Thalor]] and a cloud [[goliath-xphb|Goliath]] raised by humans named [[Venitin P]], who died screaming in tongues, suggesting his mind was completely hijacked.
