## Description

Caer Thalor rises from the forest floor like a steampunk cathedral, all gears and glass, run by [[King Thorne Durell]].

[[The Council of the Cold Veil]] keeps a vault here. The note on the [[Deliverer of Diamond of 20 Monsters|hooded, masked man]] in [[00-First Session]] was an order to bring the [[Diamond of 20 Monsters]] here to be locked away; see [[Diamond of 20 Monsters Delivery]].
