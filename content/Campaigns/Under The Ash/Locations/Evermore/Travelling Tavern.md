## Description

A flying tavern (yes, literally a flying ship). It is a well known, fun attraction which travels from region to region. It usually stations in 5 or 6 different locations per year for about 1 week to 1 month at a time. It is an extremely means of transit as you fly above the danger.

[[Jimmothy]] mentioned there is one near [[Mazca’Tan]].

We have three tickets. [[Reginald Cornfield|Reginald]] gave them to us in exchange for the [[copper-dragon|Copper Dragon]] horn in [[11-Land Mermaid#Setting up camp]]. [[V]] later told us to "enjoy" them, which is not comforting.
