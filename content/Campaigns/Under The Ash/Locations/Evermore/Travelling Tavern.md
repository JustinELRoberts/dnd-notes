## Description

A flying ship tavern that travels between regions, stopping at 5 or 6 locations a year for 1 week to 1 month each. A safe way to travel, since it flies above the danger.

[[Jimmothy]] mentioned there is one near [[Mazca’Tan]].

We have three tickets. [[Reginald Cornfield|Reginald]] gave them to us in exchange for the [[copper-dragon|Copper Dragon]] horn in [[11-Land Mermaid#Setting up camp]]. [[V]] later told us to "enjoy" them.
