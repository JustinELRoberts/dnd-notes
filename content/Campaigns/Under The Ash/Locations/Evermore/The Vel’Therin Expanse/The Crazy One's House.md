## Description

[[The Crazy One]]'s home, on the north end of [[Myrrh’s Hollow South]]. We found it in [[12-The Executioner#At The Crazy One's House]] using the brass key he gave us before he died.
Whoever left, left in a hurry. There was a layer of ash across the floor and everything was gone except a single untouched painting of his family.

## What we found

- The painting is a portal. [[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]] climbed in and found a small boxed room holding a golden chest, which the brass key opened. Inside was [[The Mirror Of What Is]]
- [[Theodore Shellington|Theodore]] was [[counterspell-xphb|Counterspell]]ed when he tried to cast [[detect-magic-xphb|Detect Magic]]
- [[V]] was in the basement working glyphs into a ritual circle; see [[12-The Executioner#In the basement]]
