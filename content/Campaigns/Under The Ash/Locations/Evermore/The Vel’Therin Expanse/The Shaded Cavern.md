## Description

South of [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Myrrh's Hollow/index|Myrrh’s Hollow]]. [[Jimmothy]] passed along a rumor that one of the [[Three Wizards]]' apprentices was hiding here, which is why we ran here when [[V]] set [[The Executioner]] on us.
The rumor was right. [[The Blue Apprentice]] lives in a house here.
We killed [[The Executioner]] outside it in [[12-The Executioner#At The Shaded Cavern]].
