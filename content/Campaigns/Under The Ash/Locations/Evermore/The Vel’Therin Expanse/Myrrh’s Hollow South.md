## Description

The town south of [[Myrrh’s Hollow]], two days' ride from the [[Blue Cross Bridge]]. [[The Crazy One]] lived here; his address was written on the back of the letter in his pocket.
It was cut off from the rest of the expanse for about a week while the land mermaid held the [[Blue Cross Bridge]], stranding its residents on the far side.
[[The Crazy One's House]] sits on the north end of town. We warned the locals to evacuate before [[The Executioner]] arrived.
