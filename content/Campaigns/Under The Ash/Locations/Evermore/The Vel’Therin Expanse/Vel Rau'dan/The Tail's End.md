## Description

Our tavern and bastion in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Vel Rau'dan/index|Vel Rau'dan]], built on the lot where the [[Starlight Inn]] [[mimic]] used to be. We bought it from [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/NPCs/Rod|Rod]] in [[07-Tavern Owners#Back at the real estate place]] for 225,000 gold.
It is a small building with elite furnishings, elite staff, and a bastion with four facilities (Arcane Study, Library, Garden, Armory) plus a basement. We open on day 24. Expected income is 3,375g/week at our current 4.5x reputation multiplier, which caps at 10x.
