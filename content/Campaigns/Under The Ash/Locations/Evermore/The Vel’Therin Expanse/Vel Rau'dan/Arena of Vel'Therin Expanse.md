## Description

A fighting arena in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Vel Rau'dan/index|Vel Rau'dan]]. We killed (an old, weakened) [[behir|Behir]] here. On the outside, there are three entrances, each for different purposes:

1. To participate in fighting
2. To observe the fighting
3. The prison where fighters are held

There are three tiers of monsters that one can fight here, each tier providing a range of betting odds:

- A: 50-100:1
- B: 10-45:1
- C: 5-10:1
- D: 0.25-5:1
