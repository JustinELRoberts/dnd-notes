## Description

The town south of [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Myrrh's Hollow/index|Myrrh’s Hollow]], two days' ride from the [[Blue Cross Bridge]]. [[The Crazy One's House]] is on the north end of town.
It was cut off for about a week while the land mermaid held the [[Blue Cross Bridge]]. We warned the locals to evacuate before [[The Executioner]] arrived.
