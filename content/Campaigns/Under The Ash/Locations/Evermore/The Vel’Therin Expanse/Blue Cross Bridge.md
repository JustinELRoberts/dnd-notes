## Description

A bridge on the road between [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Vel Rau'dan/index|Vel Rau'dan]] and [[Myrrh’s Hollow South]]. The only other crossing adds three days of travel.
The 50 foot high barricade across it was the land mermaid's lair; we killed it in [[11-Land Mermaid#Killing the land mermaid]]. We think it was a man who lived under the bridge and evolved over time.
