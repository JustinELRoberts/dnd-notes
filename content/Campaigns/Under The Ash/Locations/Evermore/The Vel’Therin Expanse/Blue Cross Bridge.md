## Description

A bridge on the road between [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Vel Rau'dan/index|Vel Rau'dan]] and [[Myrrh’s Hollow South]]. The only other crossing adds three days of travel.
[[Reginald Cornfield|Reginald]] warned us it was blocked off. When we arrived there were guards turning travelers away and a 50 foot high, 10 foot deep barricade across the span.
The barricade was the land mermaid's lair. [[Callie Dawnsong|Cal]] used [[suggestion-xphb|Suggestion]] to clear the guards, [[Raelor]] found the rope that opens it like a door, and we killed the siren inside. See [[11-Land Mermaid#Killing the land mermaid]].
The lair had evolved multiple times to get to that point. We deduced a man used to live under this bridge, and that he is what became the land mermaid.
