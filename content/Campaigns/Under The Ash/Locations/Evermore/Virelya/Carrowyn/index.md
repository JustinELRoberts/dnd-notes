## Description

A city in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Virelya/index|Virelya]], on the border with [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Karvok Thuldor/index|Karvok Thuldor]].

Both [[V]] and [[The Blue Apprentice]] insisted we come here, independently, in the same session. [[V]] says the next artifact is here; [[The Blue Apprentice]] says it's where "the story" says we're supposed to be. See [[Find the Artifact in Carrowyn]].

We arrived on day 20 aboard [[Ferrar's Airship]]. It is a circular city of five sections. [[The Mud District]] in the north is the modest one, and the prettiest; the other four are wealthy, and the south, holding [[The Citadel of Carrowyn]] and [[King Valecrest|the king]], is the wealthiest. Everything here is expensive. It is a bit of a retirement city.

## Notes

- It is usually hot. It was cold enough when we arrived to cost us a CON save and 3 damage
- The cold has been building for months, but the extreme cold began only days before we got there
- The newspaper boy: "About a week ago we saw giant red eyes in the sky, then they just went away"
- The [[CFN Headquarters|CFN]] newspaper is printed in [[The Mud District]]. Its headlines were all [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Iskar’Thul/index|Iskar’Thul]]: frost waves, an ancient roar, and the city's fear of having to choose between [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Iskar’Thul/index|Iskar’Thul]] and [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Karvok Thuldor/index|Karvok Thuldor]]
- [[The Sabertooth Tavern]] has a way into the [[Feywild]] in its basement
