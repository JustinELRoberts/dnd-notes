## Description

A city in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Virelya/index|Virelya]], on the border with [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Karvok Thuldor/index|Karvok Thuldor]].

[[V]] and [[The Blue Apprentice]] both sent us here; see [[Find the Artifact in Carrowyn]].

A wealthy, expensive retirement city in five sections; see Places below.

## Notes

- It is usually hot. It was cold enough when we arrived to cost us a CON save and 3 damage
- The cold has been building for months, but the extreme cold began only days before we got there
- The newspaper boy: "About a week ago we saw giant red eyes in the sky, then they just went away"
- The [[CFN Headquarters|CFN]] newspaper headlines were all [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Iskar’Thul/index|Iskar’Thul]]: frost waves, an ancient roar, and the city's fear of having to choose between [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Iskar’Thul/index|Iskar’Thul]] and [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Karvok Thuldor/index|Karvok Thuldor]]
