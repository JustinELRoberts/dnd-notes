## Description

A small, neutral city in the center of [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/index|Evermore]] that grants passage between the feuding north and south; locals call it the Border City. We arrived at its southeastern corner in [[00-First Session]] and smuggled the [[Diamond of 20 Monsters]] through its border.
[[Theratina]] lived alone in a cave hideout on its southeastern beach, which is where we met her. Her map of [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/index|Evermore]] there had [[Mazca’Tan]] circled.
In [[07-Tavern Owners]], [[Thompson]] slaughtered its entire population. [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] saved the one survivor, a toddler [[Ferrar]] took to [[Glasscap Grove]]. [[Thompson]] turned [[Ferrar]] to stone here, and [[Theratina]] took his hand.
