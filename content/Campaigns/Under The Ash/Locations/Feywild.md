## Description

An echo of the Material Plane, layered over the same ground rather than sitting somewhere else. The geography answers to ours — where we have a hill it has a crag, where we have a wood it has an older and hungrier wood — but it is brighter, wilder, and governed by emotion instead of sense. It is [[Shadowfell]]'s opposite number: the same world, felt rather than measured.

You cross at a fey crossing, a place where the veil is thin. Most only open on a condition — a full moon, a particular dawn, or someone carrying the right thing — and a crossing closes for good if the land at either end is changed enough. The one we know is the circle of sugar in the basement of [[The Sabertooth Tavern]] in [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Virelya/Carrowyn/index|Carrowyn]]: a pocket of dough, a pat on the head, and in you go. [[The Laughing Root]] is on the other side.

Time does not keep faith with ours. A day there can be an hour or a year back here; we lost three days on a single afternoon. Leaving can cost you the memory of it as well — a DC 10 WIS save, which fey and anything with Fey Ancestry pass automatically, and which only leaves the memories hazy even when you make it.
