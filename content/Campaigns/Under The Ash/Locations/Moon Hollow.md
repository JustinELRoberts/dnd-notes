## Description

The sanctuary [[Ferrar]] keeps, and the home of [[The Red Maw]]. [[Glasscap Grove]] sits inside it.
Per [[Ferrar]] in [[05-Shopping Spree#A conversation with Ferrar]], [[Glasscap Grove]] used to be a sanctuary guarding a powerful artifact until [[The Silent Fist]] ran through it and one person killed everyone there. Decades later one of the old members came back and cured the grove, and Moon Hollow was created around it.
[[Ferrar]] claims to shelter 45,000 wolves here, plus widows, orphans, and elderly displaced by war and famine. He offered it to [[Theratina]] as a stable home.
