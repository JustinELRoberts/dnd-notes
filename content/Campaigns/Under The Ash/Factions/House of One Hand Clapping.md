## Description

A monastery of monks practicing the shadow arts, where [[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]] was trained in the [[monk-way-of-shadow|Way of Shadow]] and taught shadow weaving. Its location is unknown; those who find the cloister do so by not searching for it.
Its monks are sought after as spies and assassins. How contracts are made is unknown, but coin changes hands. [[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]] left after becoming disillusioned with the secrecy and the unknown purpose behind those contracts.
Per the [[CFN Headquarters|CFN]] newspaper, [[The Silent Fist]] are currently in discussions with them, and [[King Valecrest]] is waiting on the outcome.
