## Description

Ancient beings. Often known as saviors of the world. They are [[The Unknown]]'s opposition. They have the colors blue, red, and yellow. Each has their own personality and their own types of apprentices. They are ageless beings who spawned into existence during the reign of [[The Unknown]]. Nobody has seen them since [[The Unknown]]'s locks were sealed.

## What we know

- Their apprentices are still around. We met [[The Blue Apprentice]] at [[The Shaded Cavern]] in [[12-The Executioner#At The Shaded Cavern]]
- Per [[The Blue Apprentice]], they are the ones who know exactly what is going to happen
- They spoke to [[Eisuke]] and his group in a dream 190 years from now, which is what started their quest
- [[The Crazy One]]'s last words were "The wizards, they..."
- Per [[V]]'s [[The Letter to Grindledark]], "the wizards and their little rebellion" are also moving on the artifacts
