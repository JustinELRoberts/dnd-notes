## Description

A group we first heard of through the [[gnome]]s at the [[CFN Headquarters]] in [[13-Full Moon#At the CFN Headquarters]]. They are going to be the new leaders and will somehow replace [[The Silent Fist]]. They are almost certainly led by [[V]]. Their mark is an eye ringed in thorns. [[The Lawnkeeper]] wears it on his back.

## What we know

- The [[gnome]]s are stuck on a puzzle of theirs they cannot finish, because the missing piece is at [[The Laughing Root]] and they are unwelcome there
