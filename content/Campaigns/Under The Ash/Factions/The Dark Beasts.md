## Description

One of the three [[Sabertooth Beasts]] factions, from [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Twin Veins/index|The Twin Veins]].

From what we hear, they seem to have a lot of enemies. We learned in [[05-Shopping Spree#A conversation with Malar]] herself does not like them. Moreover, [[Ferrar]] told us in [[05-Shopping Spree#A conversation with Ferrar]] that:

> [!quote] The dark beasts go where they want, kill who they please, and no one challenges them.

and that he ([[Ferrar]]) is trying to track down [[Vorick Bale]].
