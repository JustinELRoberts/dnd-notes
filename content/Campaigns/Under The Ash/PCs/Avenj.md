## Description

Created by the [[The Council of the Cold Veil|Council of the Cold Veil]] as a means of teaching the young students in the tower of [[Darnholt’s Eye]] about magic. He grew up learning and passing his knowledge down. Currently, he is away from [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/Iskar’Thul/index|Iskar’Thul]] on an adventure to hone his craft in the real world. Through his travels, he works in local alchemist shops by day, and wanders the streets at night searching for new animation subjects. It's been 4 years since he was sent away. He was the first [[warforged-efa|Warforged]] made by the council that has made it to the exploratory stage of the “project” he was made for. Which means so far, he is the most successful warforged created by [[Eira-Kaldran]].

## His death

[[The Unknown]] [[power-word-kill-xphb|Power Word Kill]]ed him in the [[Dimensional Blue Arena]] on day 11, as a lesson to the rest of us.

He was offered a second chance by a creature with thousands of eyes and chose red on a 50/50. He chose wrong. [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] and [[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]] both tried to trade their own lives for his; [[Theratina]] put them to sleep to stop them. Before his spirit departed, he told us not to let [[The Council of the Cold Veil|Council of the Cold Veil]] get the [[notebook with a sleeping face on its cover]].

[[Yharov Stonebrow|Yharov]] forged a blade out of his body to kill [[The Unknown]] with. His remains are in the basement of [[The Tail's End]] until we build him a shrine.
