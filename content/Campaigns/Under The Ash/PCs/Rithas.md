## Description

A pale, hooded man from the [[Shadowfell]]. He had been coming by [[The Tail's End]] every evening looking for us, and finally caught us there in [[10-Meeting Rithas#At The Tail's End]]. He introduced himself by tossing a crumpled copy of [[Lost Experiment]] at us.

He has no love for [[The Council of the Cold Veil|The Council of the Cold Veil]]. They are stealing some kind of power from his people.

He and [[Roz Rozum|Roz]] got into a scuffle almost immediately, but they worked it out. He fought the two C tier dragons with us that night.
