## Description

A player character from another campaign in the same world, dropped into ours. He is from 190 years in our future.
Roughly 4 feet tall, with two tails, red eyes, plum colored fur, and a full head of hair. He showed up confused at the bar of [[The Tail's End]] in [[11-Land Mermaid#At The Tail's End]]. [[Callie Dawnsong|Cal]] dug through his memories, confirmed his story, and vouched for him; on their word we let him travel with us.
He appears and vanishes without warning — he disappeared crossing the [[Blue Cross Bridge]] and again as we left [[The Shaded Cavern]].

## What we know

- He knew what [[The Executioner]] was on sight
- He recognized [[The Realm Jumper]] in [[The Mirror Of What Is]]
- He has never heard of [[Campaigns/Under The Ash/Locations/Evermore/The Vel’Therin Expanse/Myrrh's Hollow/index|Myrrh’s Hollow]], which suggests it doesn't survive the coming war
- The [[Three Wizards]] spoke to him and his group in a dream; that is what started their quest
