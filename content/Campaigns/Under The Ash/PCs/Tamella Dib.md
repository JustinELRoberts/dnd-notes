## Description

**Tamella Dib** (Call her Tam) is a human woman, who seems in her early 20s, has blond hair, glasses and lots of weird decorate bits (combs, coins, shiny sparkly things) somewhere on her person or stuck in her hair. She was has spent the last several years with an old witch, where she learned how to write well, hone her abilities and train her little bit of magic as she crafts tiny tools. She's an alchemist with a specialty in maps making. Recently (within the last month), the witch that'd been raising her for the last 5+ years passed away. There was no witch to take her place and Tam definitely didn't have the sort of healing type skills people might have come to the witch for. So instead of sitting around on her hands, she decided to set out and sell her scribing skills elsewhere. On her first evening out after deciding on her plan to leave she meets and old friend at the nearest tavern and explains her plans. He also needs to get out of his house for a while and adventure, so they decide to travel together... where they'll eventually meet everyone else.
