Buried within the Land Mermaid's swollen gills are the magical Earrings of Control, the artifacts that transformed the creature.

Whenever the Land Mermaid forces a creature to make a saving throw against being Charmed, Controlled, Confused, or forced to attack an ally, that creature has disadvantage on the first saving throw it makes against the effect.

Tear Out the Earrings

The earrings can be specifically targeted.

Each Earring has:

- AC
- HP
- Immune Psychic and Poison damage

Destroying one earring reduces the Land Mermaid's Spell Save DC by 2.

If both earrings are destroyed:

- It loses Earrings of Control
- It cannot cast Dominate Person
- It cannot use Love Me Instead
- Creatures currently Charmed by it immediately repeat their saving throws
